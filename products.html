FOOLPROOF KITCHEN GUIDE

How To Make Soft, Puffed Rotis

Follow these 3 simple steps to make rotis that puff high on the tawa and stay soft in lunch boxes all day.

1

Add Extra Water & Rest 15 Mins

Knead with slightly more warm water than standard flour. Let the dough rest covered for 15 minutes to allow the natural grain proteins to absorb moisture fully.

2

Rolls Easily & Puffs on Tawa

Roll smoothly with light flour dusting. Place on a hot iron tawa for 30–40 seconds per side, then touch the open flame for an instant balloon puff.

3

Stays Soft for 8+ Hours in Tiffin

Soft, moist, and tender even when packed in school or office lunchboxes. Zero brittleness, zero rubbery chew.
