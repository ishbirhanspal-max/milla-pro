KITCHEN TESTED & APPROVED

Soft Roti Cooking Steps

Rolls smoothly, puffs like a balloon on the tawa, and stays tender and fresh for lunch tiffins.

1

Add Extra Water & Rest 15 Mins

Due to the higher natural protein density and dietary fiber, add slightly more warm water when kneading. Allow dough to rest covered for 15 minutes to let the natural gluten relax.

2

Rolls Easily & Puffs on Tawa

Dust lightly with dry atta and roll evenly. Cook on a medium-hot tawa for 30–40 seconds per side, then touch the flame to see it puff completely just like standard chakki rotis.

3

Stays Soft for 8+ Hours in Tiffin

No dryness, no rubbery texture. Wrap in a cotton cloth or foil. Rotis stay soft, moist, and delicious for lunch boxes, school tiffins, and travel throughout the day.
