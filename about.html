OUR HERITAGE & PHILOSOPHY

Real Food. Real Nutrition. Real Progress.

Why did we create MILLA PRO? Because 80% of Indian diets lack essential protein, and ultra-processed chemical whey powders were never meant to be the answer.

Why MILLA PRO?

Good nutrition shouldn't have to be complicated, expensive, or alien to Indian households. For generations, the roti has been the beating heart of Indian dinner tables — warm, comforting, and unifying. But standard market wheat flour provides only ~10g to 11g of protein per 100g, forcing fitness enthusiasts, growing teenagers, and active adults to turn to chemical whey protein powders.

Whey protein tubs cost upwards of ₹2,400 to ₹4,000 per kg, frequently cause stomach bloat, digestive gas, and hormonal acne breakouts, and isolate the individual with a synthetic shaker bottle while the rest of the family eats regular food.

MILLA PRO™ High-Protein Wheat Atta was born at A K FOODS in Chembur, Mumbai, to completely transform this paradigm. By scientifically combining whole wheat, defatted soya flour, and roasted peanut flour, we created a flour that delivers an unprecedented 45g of protein per 100g (15g in every single roti), with 100% authentic whole wheat taste and zero bloating.

The 3-Ingredient Purity Commitment

We believe in complete transparency. Our packaging has nothing to hide:

  • 60% Whole Wheat & Wheat Protein: Selected golden wheat grains preserving the genuine taste, aroma, and soft puffing dynamics of classic rotis.
  • 30% Low-Fat Soya Flour: De-odourized premium plant protein packed with all 9 essential amino acids and branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs).
  • 10% Low-Fat Peanut Flour: Nutty micro-milled goodness delivering rich potassium, magnesium, and natural healthy satiety.
