Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. Clear, transparent timelines for all orders placed through millapro.in.

1. Order Processing & Dispatch Timelines

All orders of MILLA PRO™ are milled and packed fresh in our Chembur production hub:

  • Processing Window: Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST are processed and packed on the same business day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM or on Sundays/Public Holidays are processed on the next business day.
  • Dispatch Confirmation: Consignments are handed over to our courier partners within 24 to 48 hours of payment confirmation.
  • Live Tracking: As soon as your order is dispatched, you will receive an automated SMS, WhatsApp message, and email containing the live Courier tracking link and AWB number.

🚫 100% Prepaid Orders Only — Strictly No Cash on Delivery (No COD)

Because MILLA PRO is an artisanal, preservative-free whole grain product milled in fresh batches upon order placement, we do not accept Cash on Delivery. All orders must be prepaid through 100% secure Razorpay UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Cards, or NetBanking.

3. Shipping Charges & Free Delivery Threshold

  • Orders Above ₹499: 100% FREE Delivery anywhere across India (No coupon code needed). Simply choose our 5 KG Family Saver Pack (₹1,199) or two 1 KG pouches to qualify!
  • Orders Below ₹499 (Single 1 KG Pouch @ ₹249): A nominal flat shipping charge of ₹50 is applied at checkout to subsidize heavy protective packing.

4. Non-Delivery / Address Correction

Our courier partners will attempt delivery up to 3 times at your provided address and make a telephonic call to your mobile number before initiating Return to Origin (RTO). Please ensure your shipping address, landmark, and 6-digit pincode are accurate during checkout.

If you need to correct your address after placing an order, please contact our WhatsApp support immediately at +91 98765 43210 prior to courier dispatch.
