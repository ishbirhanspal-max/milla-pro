Our Process

From a single grain to your roti, in four honest steps.

Select

Wheat, soya, and peanuts; carefully chosen for native protein quality, density, and germination vigor. Every lot is thoroughly cleaned, destoned, and graded before entering the milling line.

⚙️

Dry fractionation uses air-classification to pull the naturally protein-dense portions from each grain. No water, no harsh chemical solvents, and no alcohol processing whatsoever.

Excess inert starch and non-functional bulk step aside. The protein-to-spoonful ratio surges to 46g per 100g, allowing just 3 regular rotis to fulfill 45g of your daily protein needs.
